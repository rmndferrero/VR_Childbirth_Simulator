1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW & CLINICAL OBJECTIVES
====================================================================================================
The VR Childbirth Simulator is an immersive, high-fidelity medical training application designed to
train and evaluate nursing and medical students on the aseptic preparation and sterile draping
protocol prior to vaginal delivery.

Key Educational Goals:
1. Patient Communication: Establishing rapport and securing informed consent.
2. Aseptic Technique: Correct execution of the 9-ball perineal scrub and antiseptic paint without
   cross-contamination.
3. Fluid Management: Smooth preliminary cleansing and antiseptic rinsing using a water pitcher.
4. Sterile Field Maintenance: Strict two-handed unfolding and sequential placement of sterile drapes
   with automatic sterile glove transitioning.
5. Instrument Hygiene & Safety: Zero contamination of instruments with proximity-based tray returns
   and proper disposal of bio-hazardous cotton.


====================================================================================================
2. CORE ARCHITECTURE & STATE MACHINE
====================================================================================================
The simulation is driven by two synchronized, event-driven state machines:

A. VRDemoGameManager (Master Simulation Controller)
   - File: Assets/Scripts/VRDemoGameManager.cs
   - Manages the overall simulation lifecycle (Steps 1 through 8).
   - Centralizes player score calculation, mistake logging, penalty enforcement, and JSON serialization.
   - Dispatches OnSimulationPhaseChanged events across all scene observers.

B. PerinealCareManager (Clinical Procedure State Controller)
   - File: Assets/Scripts/PerinealCareManager.cs
   - Handles the physical patient environment, HUD transitions, and procedural triggers.
   - Synchronizes UI components (FloatingPokeDialogueUI, CleaningProgressUI, StrokeGuideUI, DrapingGuideUI).

Enum Definitions:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SimulationPhase / PerinealCareState:
  - STEP_1_TALK_TO_MOTHER          (Step 1: Patient Informed Consent)
  - STEP_2_PRELIMINARY_WATER_WASH  (Step 2: Preliminary Perineal Wash 0-100%)
  - STEP_3_IODINE_7_5_SCRUB        (Step 3: 7.5% Povidone-Iodine 9-Ball Technique)
  - STEP_4_ANTISEPTIC_RINSE        (Step 4: Water Rinse of Antiseptic 0-100%)
  - STEP_5_IODINE_10_PAINT         (Step 5: 10% Povidone-Iodine Surgical Paint)
  - STEP_6_STERILE_DRAPING         (Step 6: 4-Zone Sterile Draping with Blue Gloves)
  - STEP_7_SETUP_TABLE             (Step 7: Mayo Table Setup - Modular Placeholder)
  - STEP_8_FINAL_SUMMARY           (Step 8: Final Score & Mistakes Summary Evaluation)

3. THE 8-STEP CLINICAL SIMULATION SEQUENCE (DETAILED BREAKDOWN)
====================================================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: TALK TO MOTHER / INFORMED CONSENT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- UI: FloatingPokeDialogueUI attached to DialogueCanvas bedside.
- Behavior:
  * On simulation launch, a floating prompt appears: "Talk to the Mother".
  * Poking the button displays dialogue choices for explaining the procedure to the mother.
  * Mother responds with gentle voice/text acknowledgments.
- Scoring Rules:
  * Optimal choices: 0 penalty.
  * Non-optimal/abrupt choices: Deducts penalty points (e.g. -5 pts) and logs communication mistake.
- Completion: Upon finishing dialogue, automatically advances to Step 2.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: PRELIMINARY WATER WASH
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- UI: CleaningProgressUI displays "Step 2: Preliminary Water Wash" with a continuous 0% -> 100% bar.
- Behavior:
  * Player picks up the water pitcher from Table 3.
  * Tilting the pitcher beyond 45 degrees emits water particles (PitcherPour.cs).
  * Spherecast downward checks if water stream intersects mother perineal anatomy.
  * While pouring on target, progress smoothly fills from 0% to 100%.
- Scoring Rules:
  * Dropping pitcher on floor/bed: -5 pts penalty + safety snapback.
- Completion: When progress reaches 100%, automatically advances to Step 3.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: 7.5% POVIDONE-IODINE SCRUB (9-BALL TECHNIQUE)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- UI: StrokeGuideUI appears overhead tracking Strokes 1 through 9.
- Behavior:
  * Player picks up Pickup Forceps & Handling Forceps.
  * Pickup Forceps grabs a cotton ball from the 7.5% Iodine Jar.
  * Cotton is transferred from Pickup Forceps to Handling Forceps.
  * 3D blue box guides (StrokeAreaVisualGuide.cs) appear on active landmarks ONLY when holding
    handling forceps equipped with cotton.
  * 9 Anatomical Landmarks in order:
    1. Mons Pubis (Right to Left)
    2. Right Labium Majus (Top to Bottom)
    3. Left Labium Majus (Top to Bottom)
    4. Right Labium Minus (Top to Bottom)
    5. Left Labium Minus (Top to Bottom)
    6. Right Inner Thigh / Groin
    7. Left Inner Thigh / Groin
    8. Perineum to Fourchette
    9. Anus (Direct Dab/Pat dwell >= 0.35s)
  * Completing a stroke leaves a washable translucent LIGHT GREEN puddle (WashableIodinePuddle.cs).
  * 1-Cotton-Per-Stroke Rule: Cotton ball is spent (isUsed = true) immediately upon stroke completion.
    Player must discard it into the waste basin and grab a fresh cotton ball.
  * Completed zones turn light green and are IGNORED (they will not flash red or re-trigger mistakes).
- Scoring Rules:
  * Out-of-order stroke on uncompleted zone: Flashes glowing RED, logs mistake, deducts -5 pts.
  * Dropping cotton or forceps on floor: Deducts -5 pts.
- Completion: Upon completing Stroke 9, automatically advances to Step 4.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: ANTISEPTIC RINSE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- UI: CleaningProgressUI displays "Step 4: Antiseptic Rinse" with a 0% -> 100% progress bar.
- Behavior:
  * Player tilts the water pitcher to pour clean water over the 9 light-green puddles.
  * As water hits each puddle, its opacity fades smoothly to 0 (WashableIodinePuddle.WashWithWater).
  * Progress bar dynamically calculates remaining cleanliness across all 9 puddles.
- Scoring Rules:
  * Dropping pitcher on floor: -5 pts.
- Completion: When all light green puddles reach 100% washed, automatically advances to Step 5.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5: 10% POVIDONE-IODINE SURGICAL PAINT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- UI: StrokeGuideUI displays "Step 5: 10% Povidone-Iodine Antiseptic Paint (Surgical Prep)".
- Behavior:
  * Player uses Pickup Forceps to grab cotton from the 10% Iodine Jar, transfers to Handling Forceps.
  * Player performs the 9-stroke surgical painting sequence.
  * Each completed stroke activates a PERSISTENT DARK AMBER COATING (WashableIodinePuddle.Apply10Coating)
    that remains permanently on the patient for surgical delivery.
- Scoring Rules:
  * Out-of-order stroke: -5 pts + Red Flash.
  * Dropping tools/cotton: -5 pts.
- Completion: Upon completing Stroke 9, automatically advances to Step 6.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6: STERILE DRAPING
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Hand Visuals: Player hands automatically equip sterile blue surgical gloves (PlayerHandMaterialManager.cs).
- UI: DrapingGuideUI displays "Step 6: Sterile Draping of the Mother" with a 4-step progress tracker.
- Behavior:
  * MotherDrapingManager activates 4 static soft blue holographic guides on the patient:
    Zone 1: Under Buttocks
    Zone 2: Abdomen / Lower Suprapubic
    Zone 3: Left Thigh & Leg
    Zone 4: Right Thigh & Leg
  * Two-Handed Grab Rule: Player must pick up and unfold the dry linen from Table 1 using BOTH hands.
    Attempting to place with one hand displays a warning and rejects placement.
  * Correct placement: Replaces the blue guide with a permanent green drape mesh, plays a chime,
    and automatically spawns fresh linen at Table 1.
  * Wrong placement (out-of-sequence): Flashes glowing RED, plays error sound, deducts -5 pts,
    and returns linen to Table 1.
- Completion: When all 4 drapes are placed, automatically advances to Step 7.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 7: SETUP TABLE (MAYO PREP PLACEHOLDER)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- UI: Floating prompt on DialogueCanvas: "Step 7: Setup Table (Mayo Prep) - Tap to Proceed".
- Behavior:
  * Modular placeholder step designed for future Mayo table instrument arrangement logic.
  * Tapping the button validates the step and advances to Step 8.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 8: FINAL SUMMARY & MISTAKE EVALUATION UI
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- UI: FloatingPokeDialogueUI renders the comprehensive Clinical Evaluation Card:
  1. Header: "CLINICAL EVALUATION SUMMARY"
  2. Final Score: Displays calculated score out of 100 with grade:
     - 85% to 100%: PASS (EXCELLENT)
     - 70% to 84%:  PASS
     - Below 70%:   NEEDS PRACTICE
  3. Mistake Breakdown:
     - If mistakes made: Lists every individual violation and points lost.
       (e.g., "• Step 3: Dropped cotton on floor/unsterile surface (-5 pts)")
       (e.g., "• Step 6: Out of order Draping Order Violation (-5 pts)")
     - If 0 mistakes made: "✓ Flawless Procedure! Zero sterile violations recorded."
  4. Restart Button: VR Poke-enabled "Restart Simulation" button reloads the Unity scene to pristine state.
- Storage: Automatically writes a timestamped JSON file to persistent storage.


====================================================================================================
4. SCORING ENGINE & MISTAKE EVALUATION SYSTEM
====================================================================================================

Formula:
  Final Score = Mathf.Clamp(100 - Total Deductions, 0, 100)

Deduction Matrix (Standard Rule-Based Values):
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Violation Type                                    | Penalty | Remediation / System Action
--------------------------------------------------+---------+---------------------------------------
Dropped Tool on Floor / Unsterile Bed             | -5 pts  | 1.2s safety timer -> Teleport to Table
Dropped Cotton Ball outside Waste Basin           | -5 pts  | 1.8s timer -> Log contamination
Wrong 9-Ball Stroke Sequence (Uncompleted Zone)   | -5 pts  | Glow Red for 1.25s -> Block advancement
Wrong Draping Sequence (Out of Order)             | -5 pts  | Glow Red -> Linen reset to Table 1
Non-Optimal Communication Choice (Step 1)         | -5 pts  | Patient expresses concern/nervousness
Using Spent / Dirty Cotton Ball for a new stroke  | Warning | Blocked until fresh cotton is held
One-Handed Draping Attempt                        | Warning | Blocked until held with two hands
Safe Tool Return to Table Socket (Proximity Snap) | 0 pts   | Ghost preview -> Kinematic snap lock
Safe Cotton Disposal into Bedside Waste Basin     | 0 pts   | Cotton destroyed with safe audio chime

5. INTERACTIVE HARDWARE & VR SUBSYSTEMS
====================================================================================================

1. Proximity Snap Return Sockets (ToolReturnSocket.cs & ToolItem.cs):
   - Every instrument has a home socket on Table 1 (and Table 2 when placed).
   - Proximity Detection: Bringing a tool within 20cm of its slot shows a soft blue ghost preview.
   - Releasing the tool inside the radius snaps it in place kinematically with 0 penalty.

2. Two-Handed Linen Interaction (TwoHandedLinenCloth.cs):
   - Dedicated Left and Right attach points dynamically routed to the player's controllers.
   - Multi-grab selection tracks whether both hands are holding the cloth.
   - If dropped in mid-air or dropped below 0.25m, auto-returns to Table 1 cleanly.

3. Forceps & Cotton Lifecycle (ForcepsController.cs & CottonState.cs):
   - Pickup Forceps: Hovering inside the cotton jar and holding the Primary Button spawns soaked cotton.
   - Handling Forceps: Bringing forceps tips within 5cm while holding the Primary Button transfers cotton.
   - Letting go of Primary Button releases cotton grip.

4. Modular Temporary Waste Basin (WasteBasin.cs):
   - Located at bedside / Table 2: Temporary_WasteBasin (Position: 0.35, 0.85, 2.10).
   - Trigger box catches discarded cotton and deletes it with 0 penalty.
   - Modular visualMeshSlot property allows dragging any custom 3D trashbin asset into the Inspector.

5. Dynamic Hand Glove Swapping (PlayerHandMaterialManager.cs):
   - Switches hand renderers between normal skin material and blue sterile surgical gloves during Step 6.

7. RULE-BASED VALIDATION VS. DECISION MATRIX EXPLANATION
====================================================================================================

What is currently implemented:
Your system is currently a PURE RULE-BASED VALIDATION ENGINE.
- Every check is directly computed through deterministic clinical rules in C# code.
- Advantages: Extremely fast, zero lookup overhead, robust, no missing data asset dependencies.

What a Decision Matrix would add (Optional Future Expansion):
A Decision Matrix is a data-driven table: [Step Index] x [Action/Tool] -> [Penalty, Warning, Severity].
- If you ever wish to allow non-programmers or clinical educators to edit penalty weights or
  custom warning texts via a Unity Inspector table without touching C# code, a Decision Matrix
  ScriptableObject can be layered on top of VRDemoGameManager.

8. LOCAL DATA PERSISTENCE & RECORD STORAGE
====================================================================================================
At the end of Step 8, the simulation compiles a SimulationRecord object and writes it as a formatted
JSON file to Unity's Application.persistentDataPath:

File Format: SimulationRecord_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS.json
Location on Windows:
C:\Users\<User>\AppData\LocalLow\<CompanyName>\<ProjectName>\SimulationRecord_*.json

Sample Output:
{
    "playerName": "Nurse_Trainee_01",
    "dateCompleted": "2026-09-20 16:45:00",
    "finalScore": 85,
    "mistakesMade": [
        "Step 3: Dropped Cotton on floor/unsterile surface (-5 pts)",
        "Step 6: Out of order Draping Order Violation (-5 pts)",
        "Step 1: Improper communication with patient (-5 pts)"
    ]
}
